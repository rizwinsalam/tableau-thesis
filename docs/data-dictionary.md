# Data Dictionary — synthetic_sales.csv

- **Order Date**: Date of order (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Order ID**: Unique order identifier
- **Customer ID**: Pseudonymous customer identifier
- **Segment**: Customer segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- **City**: Customer city
- **Region**: Geographic region (North, South, East, West)
- **Category**: Product category
- **Sub-Category**: Product subcategory
- **Quantity**: Units purchased
- **Unit Price**: Price per unit (USD)
- **Discount**: Discount applied (0–0.4)
- **Sales**: Net sales after discount (USD)
- **Cost**: Estimated cost of goods sold (USD)
- **Profit**: Sales minus cost (USD)
