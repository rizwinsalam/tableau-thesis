# Case Study

## Problem
Retail leadership needs a clear view of Sales and Profit performance with the ability to drill into categories and discount dynamics to identify growth levers and margin risks.

## Approach
- Generated a 1-year synthetic dataset (~14k orders) to simulate retail activity
- Defined KPIs (Sales, Profit, Margin, Avg Discount) and dashboard storyboard
- Built visuals to highlight monthly trends, category contribution, and discount–profit relationships

## Insights
- Total Sales: $1,578,052; Total Profit: $551,941; Margin: 35.0%; Avg Discount: 12.1%
- Best Month: Nov 2024 ($143,531) — seasonal uplift visible in trend
- Top Category: Furniture (33.8% of sales) — primary contributor to topline
